# Break-window statistics

The tip occupies the main area. A small break-type label, timer/controls and four statistics rows complete the window. Postpone has a +1, +2 badge counting presses in this break; the badge appears when its reminder returns, with a short CSS animation that respects reduced-motion settings. No postpone duration or scheduled-start text is shown.

| Row | Today | 7-day avg/day |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Break time | Actual timer and qualifying-idle time combined, without overlap. | Combined time across today and the previous six local dates, divided by seven. |
| Without delay | Completed breaks with zero or one Postpone, divided by all resolved breaks. Additional postponements, skips and early endings count against the percentage. | The same rate across all resolved breaks in the seven-day window. |
| Average stretch | Mean ended work stretch, excluding running breaks, qualifying idle and Pause. | Mean of all ended work stretches in the seven-day window. |
| Computer use | Tracked time while the app is running, excluding breaks, qualifying idle, Pause, suspension and schedule exclusions. | Seven-day total divided by seven, including inactive days as zero. |

A dash means no applicable breaks or work stretches yet. Computer use is an estimate, not proof of productive work. Short idle below the configured threshold remains computer use; idle cannot be excluded when monitoring is disabled. Waiting and Postpone delays count as computer use. Partial timer time is retained even if a break is later postponed or ended.

Only seven local calendar dates are retained in `stretchly-statistics.json` beside the profile's `config.json`. Older dates, lifetime totals and removed metrics are discarded on load and while running. Both the main file and its recovery backup are rewritten with the pruned data; records are never inferred for time while the app is closed. Existing daily records within seven days migrate automatically. No input or application history is recorded.

Storage remains bounded and local. Changes are combined into a five-second save, with a five-minute checkpoint and normal-Quit flush. The backup contains the same current seven-day summary; abrupt termination may lose the newest unsaved changes. Break-window figures refresh at most once per second, sharing the main-process snapshot across monitors.

Break debt uses a separate balance in the same saved file. It earns rest at the configured larger-break rate per 120 active minutes, excluding the short countdown while a larger-break window is waiting to start. Active time during a postponement still earns rest. With two 3-minute Long breaks and a 15-minute Extended break, each active minute earns 10.5 seconds of rest. Qualifying AFK and the elapsed portion of a Long or Extended timer pay down that balance. Overlap is credited once. Mini timers are excluded from the debt balance unless their time is also part of qualifying AFK. **Skipping a break from the tray is not a debt event**: the skipped window earns no rest credit, the postponement balance survives, and the outstanding amount is re-forecast onto the next larger breaks. The skip still counts as a completed-but-skipped break for Without delay %.

The app projects rest through the end of the current 120-minute work block when the schedule changes. It can add make-up time to the next three larger breaks in proportion to their base lengths. A postponement updates the forecast when the break returns. Any unpaid balance survives midnight and restart. Existing profiles start with zero debt. Time while the app is closed is excluded. The balance carries across days if you stop before taking the remaining rest.

Audit note (2026-11-14): the rate is a cumulative ledger, not a sliding two-hour history. `breakDebtRate()` divides the rest owed over one Extended cycle (one Extended plus `extendedBreakInterval - 1` Long breaks) by the fixed 120-active-minute `workBlock`. That is exactly the schedule's own average only when `extendedBreakInterval × microbreakInterval × (breakInterval + 1)` equals 120 minutes; with the current live profile (2 × 20 × 2 = 80 minutes) the ledger targets 16 minutes per 120 active minutes while the untouched schedule offers 24. The ledger is therefore a conservative floor, not an exact replay of the ideal schedule. The randomized average-check in `scripts/check-break-average.mjs` quantifies this gap; changing the rate is a separate model decision and was deliberately not done in this round.

Active work during a larger-break postponement adds at least its earned rest to the returning break, even when previous rest left a credit. While that postponement is outstanding, total debt shows at least that earned amount. This repayment is not added twice when the regular forecast already assigns more to the same break.

An early qualifying AFK restarts the current interval and preserves the upcoming larger break. AFK that overlaps its due time or ends within one minute of it can fulfill that break if it meets the base duration. An absence that begins during a running Long or Extended break and lasts at least as long as that break also fulfills it, including when the break ends while away; the absence is measured from its original wall-clock start, so an OS idle-counter reset cannot shorten it. An Extended-length absence starts a fresh reminder session. The next interval starts in full at the moment of return, and one absence can fulfill at most one scheduled break. Neither type of AFK reset clears unpaid rest.
